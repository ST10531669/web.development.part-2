About Us

Every client deserves to feel valued.

MHLONGO'S ROYAL TREATMENT

Beauty with a personal touch

Mhlongo's Royal Treatment is a beauty and personal-care business offering nail services and massage therapy. The business began by practising acrylic and gel nail services with family, friends and students, learning and improving along the way.

Over time, the business grew through practice, support and a commitment to providing a welcoming experience. The goal is to keep improving, reach more clients and build opportunities for future staff.

Our mission

To provide dependable, high-quality and professional services that help clients feel respected, comfortable and confident. We value honesty, attention to detail, individual care and lasting relationships with our clients.

Our values

  • Respect and friendly customer care
  • Professionalism and attention to detail
  • Continuous learning and improvement
  • Affordable, welcoming personal-care services
